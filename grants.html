Available Grants

The DDA administers three incentive programs to support downtown investment, building rehabilitation, and redevelopment activity across the district.

Downtown Catalyst Flex Grant Program

Gap financing for larger redevelopment projects.

This program supports major commercial, industrial, or mixed-use redevelopment projects with a minimum capital investment of $500,000. It is designed to help close funding gaps and may provide up to 10% of verified project costs.

Facade Improvement Grant Program

Matching support for exterior building improvements.

This program offers up to $5,000 in matching reimbursement for permanent exterior improvements visible from a public right-of-way. Eligible projects may include repairs, stabilization, and enhancements that improve the appearance and condition of the building.

General Business Assistance Grant Program

Support for maintenance and localized commercial improvements.

This program supports core building maintenance, permitted improvements, and other localized commercial enhancements within the district. Eligible projects may receive up to $5,000 on a 50/50 cost match basis.
