Broad Street Redevelopment

Request for Qualifications

The City of Griffin Downtown Development Authority is seeking qualifications from developers, investors, and project teams interested in redeveloping the former Heck Chapel, County Jail, and surrounding East Broad Street properties in Griffin, Georgia.

With the Courtyard by Marriott development underway on the Historic City Hall property, this area presents an opportunity to consider new investment and long-term improvements that support downtown growth and community value.

The DDA welcomes proposals that advance redevelopment planning for the East Broad Street corridor.
