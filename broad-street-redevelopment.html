Broad Street Redevelopment

Reimagining Heck Chapel, E. Broad Street, and County Jail.

{% include hero-contact-card.html %}

Request for Qualifications

The Griffin Downtown Development Authority is seeking proposals from qualified developers, investors, and teams that will help us reimagine the former Heck Chapel, County Jail, and surrounding E. Broad Street environment in Griffin, Georgia.

With the development of the Courtyard by Marriott on the front of the Historic City Hall property, we have the opportunity to partner with a first-class development team to create a quality project that accentuates the architectural aesthetics of the area and adds to the quality of life for Griffin residents.

We welcome strong proposals to help us consider the possibilities for transforming the E. Broad Street area.
