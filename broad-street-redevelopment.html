Project overview

The Griffin Downtown Development Authority is seeking proposals from qualified developers, investors, and project teams interested in reimagining the former Heck Chapel, County Jail, and adjacent East Broad Street properties in Griffin, Georgia.

With the Courtyard by Marriott underway adjacent to Historic City Hall, the area presents a rare opportunity to partner with a first-class development team to create a high-quality project that enhances the architectural character of the district and improves the quality of life for Griffin residents.

Site context and goals

The East Broad Street corridor is a historic focal point of downtown Griffin and remains central to the community's long-term revitalization strategy. Heck Chapel, a landmark Gothic Revival structure built in 1880, anchors the streetscape and deserves adaptive reuse that honors its heritage while serving contemporary community needs. The surrounding properties, including the former County Jail and adjacent vacant lots, represent an exceptional opportunity to create a cohesive, mixed-use destination that drives economic activity and cultural significance.

The DDA values proposals that demonstrate a commitment to historic preservation, architectural excellence, and genuine public benefit. Successful concepts will thoughtfully integrate the existing structures with new development, create vibrant public spaces, and establish lasting partnerships with the City and local stakeholders to ensure the project's long-term success and community impact.

Submission instructions

How to respond

Responses should be clear, detailed, and grounded in the site context. The proposal should explain the specific development opportunity being pursued, the team behind the project, the intended use and design approach, and the financial and implementation framework for moving the concept forward.

  1. Identify the portion of the project being proposed, including Heck Chapel, adjacent lots, County Jail, or a combined development strategy.
  2. Describe the project team and include relevant qualifications, firm experience, and resumes for key participants.
  3. Outline the proposed use, concept, and design narrative for the redevelopment and historic preservation strategy.
  4. Provide the proposed purchase price and financing framework for the site.
  5. Include a realistic development schedule with major milestones and an estimated completion timeline.
  6. Summarize the proposed project budget, anticipated improvements, and cost assumptions.
  7. Provide exterior elevations, site plans, and visual depictions that respect the historic character of the buildings.
  8. Explain how the project enhances the surrounding downtown context and complements the adjacent City Hall and Marriott development.
  9. State the type of public or agency assistance required to make the project feasible.
  10. Include a structural engineer review or sign-off for proposed work affecting Heck Chapel.
